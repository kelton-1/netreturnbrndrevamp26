# Conversion And Discovery Blog Plan

Date: 2026-05-27

## Working Premise

The blog should become a conversion and discovery asset, not a passive archive.

Shopify currently reports 183 published articles, and the live blog sitemap also exposes 183 article URLs. That does not mean 183 articles are actively helping shoppers. Most of the library is old testimonial/news content from 2013-2020, while only a small recent group has modern buyer-intent SEO value.

The correct move is not to turn everything off. The correct move is to treat the existing library as raw material:

- Keep useful live posts live.
- Use older posts as proof, internal-link support, customer story material, and topic inspiration.
- Refresh or consolidate posts that have buyer intent.
- Avoid investing in posts that are only historical announcements unless they support trust or product proof.

## Blog Asset Tiers

### Tier 1: Conversion Guides

These are the posts that should carry the most weight. They answer shopping questions and should drive readers toward products, collections, compare pages, or lead capture.

Current examples:

- Golf net size guide.
- Ball return guide.
- Indoor simulator bay setup guide.
- Garage/basement golf setup guide.
- Pro Series vs Home Series comparison.
- Golf mat buying guide.

Treatment:

- Refresh first.
- Add images, comparison tables, product links, and FAQs.
- Make them visible from relevant collection/product pages.
- Use them as pillar pages for topic clusters.

### Tier 2: Discovery Articles

These attract early-stage shoppers who are still learning what kind of setup they need.

Current examples:

- Launch monitor practice sessions.
- Home golf simulator accuracy.
- Multi-sport net uses.
- Improve your handicap indoors.
- Practice drills.

Treatment:

- Connect each post to a next step: choose a net, compare setups, shop a collection, or read a related guide.
- Add internal links from these posts into Tier 1 conversion guides.
- Use them for email/social repurposing.

### Tier 3: Proof Library

This is where the 150 testimonial posts matter. Most should not become standalone SEO targets, but they can support trust.

Examples:

- Teams, schools, coaches, pros, military, facilities, and customer setups.
- Bryson DeChambeau proof.
- SkyTrak, Foresight, simulator, and institutional credibility.

Treatment:

- Mine the best stories for quotes, case studies, proof modules, and internal links.
- Build “customer setup” or “trusted by” hub pages if useful.
- Link proof into conversion guides where it answers buyer doubt.
- Do not spend equal refresh effort on every testimonial URL.

### Tier 4: Archive Only

These posts may remain published but should not receive major investment unless analytics show hidden value.

Examples:

- Old contest winner announcements.
- Old quarter updates.
- Thin historical posts with no current buying intent.

Treatment:

- Leave alone unless they conflict with brand/product accuracy.
- No major SEO writing time.
- Consider redirects only when content is obsolete, duplicative, or cannibalizing better pages.

1. Confirm active blog navigation and which blogs are intentionally exposed in the header/footer.

4. Consolidate the duplicate simulator-bay article recommendation into a human-review brief.

### Week 2: Refresh The Highest-Intent Guide

Start with the article most likely to convert:

- `Golf Net Size Guide`
- or `Garage/Basement Golf Setup`
- or `Pro Series vs Home Series`

Refresh requirements:

- Clear answer in the first 150 words.
- Product fit table.
- Internal links to relevant product/collection pages.
- Internal links to 2-4 related guides.
- Featured image and descriptive alt text.
- Short FAQ section.
- Meta title and meta description.
- Human product-accuracy review before Shopify update.

## Decision Rules For Agents

When an agent reviews a blog post, it should decide which job the post has:

- `Conversion`: directly helps shoppers choose and buy.
- `Discovery`: captures early search demand and moves readers deeper.
- `Proof`: supports trust, credibility, and objections.
- `Archive`: stays live but is not worth active investment.

If the post has no clear job, the agent should recommend one of:

- merge into a better article
- redirect to a better article
- leave live as archive
- repurpose as proof inside another page
- rewrite as a new buyer-intent article

## What Not To Do

- Do not mass-publish generic AI posts.
- Do not rewrite all 183 posts just because they exist.
- Do not turn old posts off without checking links, rankings, and historical value.
- Do not treat testimonial posts as equal to buyer guides.
- Do not publish claims about product specs, compatibility, or professional use without verification.

## Success Metrics

The blog is carrying its weight when it improves:

- organic impressions for buyer-intent topics
- clicks into guides and product pages
- internal-link paths from articles to collections/products
- add-to-cart or lead actions from blog landing pages
- assisted conversions from blog sessions
- reusable proof assets for PDPs, collection pages, email, and ads
