## Agent SEO Standard

For every proposed article, agents should include:

- Primary keyword and search intent.
- Suggested title, URL handle, meta title, and meta description.
- Target reader and buying stage.
- Product/collection links to include.
- Existing posts to link from and to.
- Unique brand proof: product specs, customer setup, pro usage, video, testimonial, or staff expertise.
- Image requirements and alt text.
- FAQ section only when it answers real buyer questions.
- Publish/update recommendation: new post, refresh, merge, redirect, or leave alone.

## Operating Rhythm

- Monthly: refresh the Shopify export and update the index.
- Monthly: choose one new buyer-intent article and one refresh/consolidation task.
- Quarterly: review top blog pages in Search Console, update internal links, and identify articles with impressions but weak clicks.
- Before publishing: verify product details, dimensions, pricing, claims, and links against live Shopify data.
