Shop packages

Complete practice setups.

Visual weight: minimal (no background, ~16px tall total including padding). Reference: Allbirds, Aritzia, Lululemon, On Running.
  • On dark pages, text auto-switches to a light variant via CSS.
  • On collection pages, the breadcrumb sits on the small white gap between the dark header and the dark hero. Reads as a quiet transition rather than another UI band.

A2 · Slim inline, overlaid on dark hero (collection pages only)

Same as A1, but on collection pages the breadcrumb is inset over the dark hero itself (light text on dark hero). On PDPs (no hero), behaves like A1. Closer to what Aritzia does on category landing pages.
